A plugin failed to execute its goal.

This error usually comes from the plugin itself: its configuration is invalid, or
the information it read from the POM is not what it expects. The exact meaning
depends on the plugin, so consult the plugin documentation (most are listed on
https://maven.apache.org/plugins/index.html) and the goal that failed.

If the failure involves network access (download or upload of artifacts), check
your network connection, your proxy configuration, and the repositories declared
in the POM or in your settings.xml.
